NEUVTO — logo pack
==================

Mark: isometric capital "N". Transparent PNG throughout.

png/
  neuvto-mark-source.png  1024x1024 master
  neuvto-mark-1024/512/256/128/64/32.png  square, padded (not stretched)

favicon/
  favicon.ico          multi-size (16/32/48/64)
  apple-touch-icon.png 180x180

Usage
  - Keep clear space of at least 25% of the mark's width on all sides.
  - Don't recolour, rotate, stretch, or add effects.
  - On dark backgrounds use the mark as-is; it is designed for both.
  - Below 32px use favicon.ico or the 32px PNG — the 3D faces lose definition.

Brand colours
  Sky blue  #0EA5E9
  Black     #0A0A0A
  Red       #EF4444
Type: Space Grotesk (display) / Inter (body)

In the app this same mark lives at src/assets/neuvto-mark.png and public/favicon.png.
